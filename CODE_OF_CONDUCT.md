# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We want RAVENCLAW to be a serious, respectful, and safety-conscious project.
Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to act in ways that support technical honesty, operator safety, and professional collaboration.

## Expected behavior
- be respectful and clear in technical discussion
- assume good faith, but prioritize evidence over ego
- welcome careful questions and bounded disagreement
- avoid harassment, insults, dogpiling, and personal attacks
- do not encourage unsafe, unauthorized, or reckless security activity
- respect publication boundaries, redaction needs, and private reporting channels

## Unacceptable behavior
- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- publishing private credentials, tokens, target data, or sensitive artifacts without authorization
- using the project community to promote unauthorized intrusion or irresponsible disclosure
- repeated bad-faith misrepresentation of project scope, safety posture, or governance constraints

## Reporting
If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the same private reporting path described in `SECURITY.md` until a dedicated public community contact is published.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code of conduct or the repository's safety/governance posture.
