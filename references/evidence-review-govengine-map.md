# Evidence Review GovEngine Map

2026-05-20 update: Ravenclaw now has a GovEngine 0.7 evidence-review
projection helper at `engine/govengine_review_projection.py`. The helper
validates receipt-bounded evidence claims against `govengine.review` without
moving Ravenclaw finding taxonomy or SCLite review-bundle verdict authority into
GovEngine.

Dry-run receipts can support execution-truth review, but cannot support live
vulnerability claims.
